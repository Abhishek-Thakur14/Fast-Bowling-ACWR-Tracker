The method

How ACWR is calculated

ACWR=
Acute loadCurrent week total balls
÷
Chronic loadAverage of previous 4 weeks

Worked example: if Weeks 1–4 total 120, 140, 150, and 160 balls, the chronic load for Week 5 is 142.5. A Week 5 acute load of 200 gives an ACWR of 1.40 — moderate risk.

Important reminders

  • Increase gradually — aim for the 5–10% rule.
  • Avoid sudden spikes in bowling workload.
  • Listen to your body, especially soreness and fatigue.
  • Recovery is as important as training.
  • Quality > quantity — bowl with a plan.
